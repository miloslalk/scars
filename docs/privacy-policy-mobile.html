Effective date: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, used and protected when you use the When Scars (!) Become Art mobile application (the "App") for Android and iOS.

The App was developed within the Erasmus+ KA2 project "When Scars (!) Become Art" to support the emotional wellbeing of young people. We are committed to protecting your privacy and to processing your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

This policy applies to the mobile App only. The project website (whenscarsbecomeart.eu) has its own separate Privacy Policy.

1. Data controller

The data controller responsible for personal data processed through the App is:

Amaro Foro e.V.
Berlin, Germany
Email: info@amarodrom.de

2. What data we collect

2.1 Account data

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Password (managed by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your password in plain text)

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive only your name and email address from that provider. With Apple you can choose to hide your real email address.

We ask you to verify your email address. Email/password accounts that are not verified within 5 days are automatically deleted.

2.2 Optional profile data

  • Avatar photo — only if you choose to set one, using your camera or photo library, and always with your device's permission
  • Language and theme preferences

2.3 Wellbeing content you create in the App

The App is designed for self-reflection, and its core features store the content you create:

  • Mood drawing check-ins (your drawings, saved as images)
  • Journal entries
  • Body-awareness entries (the places you mark on the body outline, including region and color)
  • Feedback you give after guided exercises
  • Quotes, messages and resources you save to your library
  • Which daily messages you have opened or popped, and your daily check-in status

Please note: this content may reveal information about your emotional state and wellbeing. Such data is treated as sensitive (health-related) data under the GDPR and is processed only with your explicit consent, solely to provide the App's features to you. It is never used for any other purpose.

2.4 Technical data

  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and device platform (Android/iOS)
  • Time zone and UTC offset (so notifications arrive at the right local time)
  • Notification preferences
  • Account timestamps (creation date, last login) and email-verification status

2.5 Moderation data

If you report a message in the App, we store the report — including the reported message text and your user ID — so our team can review it.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No analytics or usage-tracking tools
  • No crash-reporting tools
  • No advertising or marketing tools
  • No precise location data (only your time zone)
  • No access to your contacts

We never sell your personal data, and we never use it for advertising.

4. Purposes and legal bases

We process your data for the following purposes:

  • To provide the App and your account (registration, login, storing your content, making it available on your devices) — performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • To store the wellbeing content you create — your explicit consent, Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR. You provide this content voluntarily and can delete it at any time.
  • To send optional push notification reminders — your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.
  • To keep the App safe (review of reported messages, abuse prevention, security) — our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • To comply with legal obligations — Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

5. Push notifications

Push notifications are optional:

  • A daily reminder at a time you choose (default 9:00 in your local time)
  • A gentle reminder if you have not opened the App for 7 days (sent at most once per 7 days)

You can switch both off at any time in the App's notification settings or in your device's system settings. Your notification token is removed when you sign out.

6. Where your data is stored

The App's backend runs on Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited): Firebase Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging and Cloud Functions. Google processes this data on our behalf as a data processor.

Data may be processed on Google servers inside and outside the European Economic Area (including the United States). Such transfers are safeguarded by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

If you sign in with Google or Apple, their handling of your data is additionally governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

7. How long we keep your data

  • Your data is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Email/password accounts that are never verified are automatically deleted 5 days after registration.
  • When you delete your account, your data is deleted immediately (see section 8).
  • Records of reported messages may be kept after account deletion where necessary for safety and audit purposes.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time directly in the App (avatar menu → settings → delete account). For your protection, you will be asked to confirm your identity first. Deleting your account removes:

  • your authentication account,
  • your entire profile and all wellbeing content (check-ins, journal entries, body-awareness entries, saved items, message history, preferences),
  • all files you uploaded (drawings and avatar photos),
  • your username entry and your devices' notification tokens.

This deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you,
  • have inaccurate data corrected,
  • have your data deleted,
  • restrict processing,
  • receive your data in a portable format,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests,
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing that happened before the withdrawal).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@amarodrom.de. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority — for us this is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).

10. Children

The App is not intended for children under 16 without the consent of a parent or legal guardian, and only where permitted by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided us personal data without such consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page; if the changes are material, we will provide notice in the App or by email.
